About LunaWake

Sleep support, with care.

We are building a bedside system that helps the night feel less managed and the morning feel more possible — without a wearable, a camera, or a louder alarm.

The team behind the bedside system

What we believe

A better morning begins with a more considered night.

Wake-up time is not one feeling.Some mornings call for a clear boundary. Some call for a little more time. LunaWake is being shaped around those real scenarios.

LunaWake sits at the bedside and follows the full journey from night to morning: winding down, sensing quietly through the night, and waking inside the window you choose. The Luna App is the configuration and reflection layer; the device remains the always-available bedside system.

Our work is guided by sleep science, translated into an experience that stays calm, legible, and honest about what it can — and cannot — tell you.

How we work

Quiet technology needs clear principles.

We care about the details that make a system feel trustworthy at 2 a.m. and useful at 7 a.m.

01 / Privacy

Notice, don’t intrude.

Contact-free sensing, no camera, and a physical privacy gate are part of the product’s foundation.

02 / Clarity

Useful, not overwhelming.

The app keeps configuration and morning reflection readable instead of turning sleep into an endless score.

03 / Honesty

Build in public with care.

We share what is ready, what is illustrative, and what still needs validation before we make stronger claims.

The team behind the build

Engineering and interaction, at the same bedside.

We are a team of senior algorithm engineers, hardware engineers, and interaction designers working across the object, the sensing system, and the moments around sleep.
