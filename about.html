About Us

We’re Lunawake. Bedside intelligence that helps you sleep better and wake up smarter. It supports the full journey from night to morning: tracking your sleep without wearables, waking you more gently at the right moment, and guiding small routines that help your schedule actually stick.

Lunawake sits on your nightstand and senses breathing and micro-movement, then uses that context to shape a more natural wake-up flow. Less shock. Less endless snoozing. And we built it around a simple truth: “wake-up time” doesn’t mean the same thing every day. Sometimes you need to get up now. Sometimes you want a little more sleep but can’t risk oversleeping. Sometimes you want to ease into clarity. Lunawake adapts to those real scenarios.

Our experience is guided by evidence-based sleep science, translated into a calmer bedside routine. The Luna App is the configuration and reflection layer; the device remains the always-available bedside system.

We’re a team of senior algorithm engineers, hardware engineers, and interaction designers. EVT is complete and DVT is in progress as we continue validating the hardware and its bedside interactions.

Lunawake uses contact-free sensing to capture overnight signals without wearables and without cameras. Its microphones support passive acoustic sensing only — not voice commands, text-to-speech, or conversation.
We’re preparing the next validation and launch steps. Privacy-first design, ongoing updates, and transparent delivery.
