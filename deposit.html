What does LunaWake do?

LunaWake reads your night from the bedside, then shapes sound, light, and room temperature around your sleep. Nothing to wear and nothing to charge.

How do you know it's accurate?

LunaWake is being benchmarked night-for-night against polysomnography (PSG) — the gold standard used in clinical sleep labs. Full-night sessions in a university sleep laboratory compare its sleep stages, breathing, and movement against PSG, epoch by epoch. Validation is ongoing, and we'll share results before launch.

Do I need a subscription?

No subscription is required for the LunaWake device. Your reservation includes 30 days of Luna Coach free while you wait.

When does LunaWake ship?

We will share the delivery schedule on launch day and in your order confirmation. Luna Coach starts when the campaign ends, before the device ships.

Why do I pay in two steps?

It keeps your risk tiny. Today, $9 locks your $229 founding price — $90 below launch day. On Sep 21, we email you a private order link; follow it to pay the rest and your order is complete. (That final step is completed through our Kickstarter campaign, at your locked price.)

Can I get my $9 back?

Yes. You can request a full refund before or after launch, for any reason. If the campaign does not go ahead, the $9 is refunded in full through the original payment method.

Why does LunaWake cost what it costs?

It's a sensing device, a light, a speaker, and an on-device processor in one object — built to run your whole night locally, without a wearable and without a camera. Most people considering LunaWake are otherwise looking at a bedside light and a tracker they'd have to wear. We've priced it as one purchase, not two.

Can I spread the payment?

Payment plans are available at checkout, including PayPal Pay in 3 and Klarna, so LunaWake can be spread over three months at no extra cost. We're also setting up an accessibility program — email us and we'll share the details.

What does it need to work?

Core functions work locally — features such as updates and backups need Wi-Fi. The planned app experience supports iOS and Android. It is designed for the one sleeper closest to the device.
