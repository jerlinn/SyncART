What does the $9 lock?

The $9 locks your Founding Backer or then-current launch-window eligibility. It is paid through Shopify, is not a Kickstarter pledge, and does not automatically transfer to or deduct from Kickstarter.

When can I get my $9 back?

You can request a full refund before Kickstarter launches. After launch, the $9 is non-refundable if you do not pledge. If the campaign fails or is canceled, the $9 is refunded in full through Shopify.

What if I pay the reservation but never pledge?

After Kickstarter launches, the $9 remains non-refundable. No private Reward price or coaching is issued unless you complete a valid pledge.

What if I cancel my pledge?

Canceling the pledge ends the private offer and does not create a new deposit refund.

What if the Kickstarter campaign fails?

If the campaign fails or is canceled, LunaWake will refund the $9 deposit in full through the original Shopify payment method.

How do I get into the deposit user group?

After payment, the group invitation and next-step information are sent by email. Use the email from your Shopify order so we can match your reservation.

Can I choose a finish now?

Yes. You can select a preferred finish here, but it is optional and does not affect payment. Confirm your final preference after reserving.
