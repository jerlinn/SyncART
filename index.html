Product mechanism · one quiet loop

How LunaWake follows the night.

While you sleep, LunaWake turns radar signals into sleep data on the device. After the night ends, that night’s record is encrypted and uploaded to build your report. In the morning, light and sound build at the time you set.

01 / On the deviceSensing becomes sleep data at the bedside.

Radar signals are processed on the device. They are motion, not images, and can’t be turned into a picture of you.

02 / Nightly recordEach night’s record is uploaded, encrypted.

It is used to create your sleep stages, morning summary, and trends. Live readings are passed to the app for display and are not stored.

03 / Your timeThe alarm starts when you set it.

Smart Wake adds a soft glow ten minutes before. It never moves the alarm.

Data boundary Radar signals are processed on the device. Each night’s sleep record is encrypted and uploaded to build your report. When a snore-like sound occurs, a clip of a few seconds is uploaded for snore detection. No other audio is recorded.

Questions, answered

Start with the important ones.

Clarity matters. Here is what LunaWake is designed to do — and where we are still learning.

Do I need to wear anything?

No. LunaWake is a bedside system, with no ring, watch, or sensor on your body.

Does LunaWake use a camera?

No. LunaWake has no camera. Its radar senses the small motion of breathing, not images. The microphones measure how loud the room is, and when a snore-like sound occurs, a clip of a few seconds is captured for snore detection. There is no speech recognition and no voice assistant. Flip the physical privacy switch and the radar and microphones stop sensing.

What does it measure?

Whether you are in bed and how you breathe, plus room temperature, humidity, and light. The device turns these into sleep data locally. After each night, the record is encrypted and uploaded to create your sleep stages, morning summary, and trends. It is not a medical diagnostic system.

Does LunaWake work offline?

For the essentials, yes, after a one-time Wi-Fi setup. Alarms, light, sound, and overnight recording keep running on the device, and records upload once you are back online. Sleep reports, Luna, and updates need a connection. LunaWake has no battery, so after a power cut it needs to reconnect to recover the time and your alarm. For an important morning, set a backup alarm.

Is it a medical device?

No. LunaWake is a consumer wellness product. It does not diagnose, treat, or prevent any condition. A comparison against PSG in a university sleep lab is in progress.

Why does LunaWake cost what it costs?

It’s a sound machine, a sunrise alarm, and a wearable-free sleep tracker in one bedside object, with an AI sleep coach on top. It is a one-time purchase, and device features and your sleep data don’t need a subscription.

Is there a subscription?

No. There are no monthly or annual fees. Device features and your sleep data stay free, and what Luna brings you each day is free. In-depth answers from Ask Luna use credits. Every LunaWake comes with 10,000 credits, and they never expire. If you run out, Luna still answers.

Is my data mine?

Yes. Your sleep data belongs to you. We don’t sell it, use it for advertising, or use it to train AI models. You can ask for an export or deletion at any time by emailing support@lunawake.ai. In-app export and deletion are in development.

Can I use the Luna App without the device?

Yes. The app can work from the sleep data in Apple Health. Device features, bedroom readings, and Rhythm check need LunaWake.
