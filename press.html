The short version

LunaWake senses the night from the nightstand — no wearable, no camera — then raises light and sound inside the wake window you choose. Contact-free sensing is being tested against technician-scored polysomnography; what is tested and what is still held back is documented on the Science page.

LunaWake is a consumer wellness product. It is not a medical device and is not intended to diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any disease.
