A clear chain of reasoning

We do not start with a technology. We start with a night.

Most people do not need another dashboard at bedtime. They need a room that becomes easier to settle into, a wake-up that feels less abrupt, and information that stays honest about what it can tell them.

Four design pillars

Every scientific idea has to become a clear product behaviour.

We explain the principle, the design choice, and the part that still needs validation.

01 / Behaviour

Borrow the principles. Do not pretend to be the treatment.

We draw on sleep-behaviour ideas associated with CBT-I, such as consistent routines and reducing unnecessary sleep pressure. LunaWake is not CBT-I therapy and does not diagnose or treat insomnia.

Product translationWake windows, repeatable wind-down cues, and less emphasis on a sleep score.Evidence status · Design rationale / validation in progress
02 / Light

Let the room change gradually.

Light is used as an environmental cue, with timing, intensity, and transition speed designed to support a calmer shift between night and morning.

Product translationWarm wind-down light and a gradual rise during the selected wake window.Evidence status · Experience testing in progress
03 / Sound

Make sound part of the environment, not another interruption.

We focus on volume, texture, timing, and change rather than promising that a particular sound can produce the same response for everyone.

Product translationGentle audio cues that can build with the wake window and remain adjustable.Evidence status · Experience testing in progress
04 / Sensing

Sense without asking you to wear another device.

Bedside signals such as presence, movement, and breathing-related patterns can support local device behaviour without a camera or skin contact.

Product translationContact-free sensing, local routines, and a physical privacy gate for acoustic sensing.Evidence status · Hardware and reference-measure validation in progress

Research in progress

A photo shows that research happened. The method shows what it means.

We are using controlled sleep-lab environments to compare LunaWake signals and behaviours with professional reference measurements. This work helps us understand what the system can support, where it is reliable, and where it should stay quiet.

01Research question

Can contact-free bedside signals be useful without becoming intrusive?

02Reference method

Professional sleep monitoring and study protocols, documented per project.

03Publication boundary

Partner name, protocol, sample, and results are published only after authorization and review.

Current statusValidation is in progress. These images are evidence of the research process, not a clinical efficacy claim.

What we can say today

Trust grows when the boundary is visible.

We can say

“Designed from sleep-behaviour and environmental principles.”

“Contact-free sensing supports the bedside routine.”

“Validation is in progress, and we will publish methods as they are cleared.”

We do not say

“Treats insomnia.”

“Accurately diagnoses sleep disorders.”

“Guaranteed to improve deep sleep.”

LunaWake is a consumer sleep companion, not a medical device. It does not diagnose, treat, or prevent disease.
