Basics

Start here for a quick, accurate overview.

What is Lunawake? ▼

Lunawake is a bedside hardware system paired with the Luna App. It senses contact-free signals, shapes light and sound around a wake routine, and remains usable locally even when the app or network is unavailable.

Why is Lunawake different from a regular alarm clock? ▼

Lunawake combines contact-free sensing, physical controls, light and sound, and a configurable wake window. It is built around observable signals and deterministic device behavior, not diagnosis or an always-on conversation.

What if I’m awake but not fully up? ▼

The device follows the current state of the night and responds to physical controls, sensing events, and the configured wake window. The app presents summaries and configuration; it does not replace the device controls.

How it Works

How sensing, timing, and guidance come together.

Does it still track sleep? ▼

It records the signals and events defined by the current interaction state machine, primarily to support local behavior and a morning summary rather than an endless metrics dashboard.

How does it work without wearables? ▼

It uses bedside radar and environmental sensing to observe contact-free signals such as movement and breathing-related patterns. No ring. No watch.

Can it wake two people? ▼

The current hardware is designed for one sleeper closest to the device. Reliable multi-person separation is not a supported capability.

Privacy & Safety

Do you use a camera or keep voice recordings? ▼

A: No camera. The microphones are used only for passive acoustic sensing, such as room noise and snoring or speech events. There are no voice commands, TTS, or conversations.

Is it a medical device? ▼

No. It’s a consumer wellness product and not intended to diagnose, treat, or prevent disease.

App & Compatibility

Is there an app? ▼

Yes. The app is used for setup, preferences, configuration, and morning summaries. It is not a replacement for the physical controls, and the device is designed to continue its core routine locally if the app or network is unavailable.

Does it work without Wi-Fi? ▼

Core functions work locally. Some features like updates and backups need Wi-Fi.

What phones are supported? ▼

iOS and Android.

How long does personalization take? ▼

The app can configure the current night’s preferences before the sleep session. The device then follows its local state machine; unfinished configuration is not silently invented during the night.
